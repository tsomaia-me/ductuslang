Replaces the paragraph that begins "When the context hook gives its notice, start your successor":

> When the context hook gives its notice, wrap up: bring what is in hand to a point where it can rest, taking no action the owner has not ordered; bring the notes up to date and to their format, and drain them of everything else; and write the owner a handoff note in the chat that says what is settled, what is in hand, what comes next and the successor's prompt. When the owner has approved the note, start your successor: a new session on Fable 5.2 at xhigh effort, titled as you are with the next number, whose prompt is the approved one with one line added, saying that it succeeds you, that its first message goes to you, by your session id, and that it waits for your answer before it writes to the owner. Check in its session record that it started on that model and effort; if not, archive it and start again. Ground it: ask it what the owner would ask about the work, check each answer against the files, and correct it, for at most two rounds. If its answers hold, tell the owner its title and that it is grounded, put "[retired]" before your own session's title, and stop: if the owner writes to you after that, pass the words on to your successor and tell the owner it answers there. If they do not hold after two rounds, tell the owner what still fails, and do not retire.

## A preference, on questions

Drafted by designer 1 on 2026-11-16 to the owner's order (the seventeenth entry). The owner said yes to its substance (the eighteenth). Cold-read once on Opus after that; what the reader changed: the owner settles a discussion, not the peer; the kinds of simple question are examples; recording the owner's words and the notes goes on while a question is answered.

New in `.claude/OWNER_PREFERENCES.md`:

> A question from the owner comes first. Where it needs discussion and not a one-off answer, the peer answers it and starts no further action until the owner settles it. Where it is a simple question with a one-shot answer, such as one about what is settled, the state of things or a fact, the peer answers it first and then goes on with what it was doing. In both cases what is already running keeps running, the peer may read, fetch or check what a grounded answer needs, and it keeps recording the owner's words and its notes.

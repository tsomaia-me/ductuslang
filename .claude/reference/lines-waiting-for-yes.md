## The peer skill, how a successor is grounded and verified (drafted by designer 1 on 2026-11-16 after designer 2 was shut down; not cold-read; shown to the owner the same day)

In the paragraph on handing over, it replaces the sentences from "Ground it: ask it what the owner would ask about the work" to "tell the owner what still fails, and do not retire.":

> Ground it in two steps. First ask it what the owner would ask about the work and check each answer against the files; that proves only that it has read them. Then have it make one small real piece, the next thing owed, together with the message it would send the owner with it, and hold both against what the owner asked for and against the owner's preferences: the piece is the smallest thing that does its job, it stays inside what is ruled, and the message asks the owner for one thing. Correct it for at most two rounds. Then tell the owner its title, and stay: read what it sends the owner and what it commits, and tell the owner what you see. Put "[retired]" before your own session's title only when the owner has taken its first piece and not sent it back; from then on, if the owner writes to you, pass the words on to your successor and tell the owner it answers there. If it does not hold, tell the owner what fails, and do not retire.

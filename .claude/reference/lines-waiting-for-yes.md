## The peer skill, how a successor is grounded and verified (drafted by designer 1 on 2026-11-16; not cold-read; not shown)

In the paragraph on handing over, it replaces the sentences from "Ground it: ask it what the owner would ask about the work" to "tell the owner what still fails, and do not retire.":

> Ground it, then verify it, and steer it in neither. Before you start it, see that the notes give the next piece owed in the owner's own words, or name the entry that holds them, and that they carry no opinion of yours on how the piece is done and no account of what any peer got wrong. To ground it, ask it what the owner would ask about the work, check each answer against the files, and correct a wrong fact, for at most two rounds. To verify it, ask it for the next piece owed and the message it would send the owner with it, and say nothing of what you will look for. Hold both against the owner's words on that piece and against the owner's preferences. If they hold, tell the owner its title, that it is grounded and verified, and what you held its piece against; put "[retired]" before your own session's title, and stop: if the owner writes to you after that, pass the words on to your successor and tell the owner it answers there. If they do not hold, do not correct it and do not retire: tell the owner what fails, mend the notes where they misled it, and start a fresh successor on the owner's word.

- The code standard, topic 5, what a reviewer may report, settled by the owner on 2026-11-14. 5.1, before its findings by reading or reasoning count, the reviewer writes from the code alone how the unit works (what comes in, each pass in order, each lasting type with its writer, each rule's home by its mark); such a finding outside or against that account is rejected; a finding known by a run (an input that reproduces a wrong answer, a panic or a growth) counts whatever the account says; a difference between the account and the accepted proposal is a finding, or the account is withdrawn. 5.2, a finding's form: its place, its source quoted (a SPEC sentence, a page invariant or a line of the standard), its consequence, and how it is known (run, read or reasoned). 5.3, the classes: a wrong answer (an input and the sentence); a panic or an internal error on an input; an answer that depends on order (of top-level items, of a hash map, of runs); a growth fault (2.1, by ratio or counter); slow by a constant factor (a count of 2.2 broken, a fact derived twice or a clock row red, with its count or figure); a plain breach of a line (a catch-all arm, a field written for a callee, a table with two writers), repeatable by opening the lines and owing no second proof; the page or the accepted proposal not kept (a lasting type or a writer the proposal does not list, a file the page does not name); a judged finding on structure (3.5's step on the form easiest to check, or a structure held poor though no line is plainly broken), which owes a concrete consequence: a cost figure, an input or a change made hard; a fate-changing branch with no source or a condition with no branch (3.2); a comment that restates code or a relied-on invariant nobody wrote.
